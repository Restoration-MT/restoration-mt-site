Privacy & data handling

What this website collects, which providers handle inquiries, and how to request a correction.

Information collected

This site does not require an account. When you follow an inquiry link, an embedded third-party HighLevel / LeadConnector form may collect information that you choose to submit, including business contact information and the problem you describe. Do not submit secrets, regulated data, classified material or sensitive customer information.

Purpose and handling

Inquiries are used to understand your request, determine a suitable next step, and respond. The form provider may process the information under its own infrastructure and applicable privacy notices. An inquiry does not automatically enroll you in unrelated marketing.

Analytics, embedded content and external sites

The site does not include a first-party tracking pixel by default. External form embeds, font delivery and linked third-party services may process technical connection data. When you leave this domain, the destination service controls its own practices.

Requests and corrections

Use the contact route to ask about information you submitted or request a correction or deletion where applicable. The governing privacy notice and lawful contracting disclosures should be confirmed with the actual legal entity before collecting additional categories of personal data.
